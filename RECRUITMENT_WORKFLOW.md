# RMC Recruitment Flow

This document explains the step-by-step recruitment journey for new members applying to the Ramanujan Mathematics Club. It's meant to give team heads a clear, high-level picture of how we induct new members.

## 1. The Application Stage
- Students interested in joining RMC fill out our online application form.
- They provide their basic details, academic information, and rank the teams they are most interested in joining.
- Once they submit the form, they receive a confirmation email welcoming them, and the core team is automatically notified of their application.

## 2. Setting Up the Tasks (For Team Heads)
- Before we test the applicants, team heads prepare up to three different assignments (tasks) for their specific team.
- You upload these tasks via your Team Dashboard so they are ready to be distributed.

## 3. Distributing Tasks to Candidates
- Through the Team Dashboard, team heads can bulk-send tasks to the students who prioritized their team (their 1st or 2nd choice).
- To keep things fair and varied, the system randomly selects one of the three tasks you prepared and emails it directly to the candidate.
- Candidates will receive an email with a secure link to view their instructions.

## 4. Candidate Task Submission
- Candidates work on their assignments and submit their results (usually via a Google Drive link) through our portal.
- Alongside their work, we ask them two short questions:
  1. Why do you want to join RMC?
  2. Why did you choose this specific team?
- Once they submit, you will be able to see their answers and their Drive link on your Team Dashboard.

## 5. Reviewing and Interviewing
- You and your team members review the submitted tasks through your dashboard.
- If a candidate's work meets your standards, you can schedule an interview right from the dashboard by providing a date, time, and Google Meet link.
- The candidate gets an automated email with the interview details.
- To make communication easier, the dashboard also gives you a quick link to message the candidate directly on WhatsApp.

## 6. Final Decision
- After conducting the interviews, team heads and the council convene to make the final selection and officially welcome the new members to the team!
