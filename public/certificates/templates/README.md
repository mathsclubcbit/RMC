# Certificate Templates

Place your Canva-exported background PNG files here.

## Naming Convention
- `mpl-participation.png` — MPL participation certificate background
- `workshop-completion.png` — Workshop completion background
- etc.

## How to export from Canva
1. Open your certificate design in Canva
2. Duplicate the page
3. On the duplicate, delete the dynamic text (paragraph + QR placeholder)
4. Keep borders, logo, signatures, decorations
5. File → Download → PNG → Download
6. Rename and place here
